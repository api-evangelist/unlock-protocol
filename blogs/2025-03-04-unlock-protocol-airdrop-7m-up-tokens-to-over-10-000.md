The Unlock Protocol Foundation is launching its next airdrop to Unlock Protocol community members, distributing over 7 million $UP tokens on Base to over 10,000 community members. The airdrop is starting March 4, 2025. A previous airdrop , from fall 2024, rewarded Unlock Protocol DAO members who migrated their UDT governance tokens from mainnet to the UP governance token on Base.
