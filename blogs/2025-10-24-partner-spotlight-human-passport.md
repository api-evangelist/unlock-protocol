A bit over a year ago, Unlock Labs integrated the Gitcoin Passport hook into the protocol. A lot has changed since then, starting with the fact that the Gitcoin Passport is now called Human Passport and has become part of human.tech and the Holonym Foundation . Human Passport has matured and is now more focused than ever on laying the foundations for an internet where people and not bots hold the keys to trust in a trustless world.
