Did you know that Unlock DAO is a decentralized autonomous organization that governs the actual protocol? These are ordinary people, like you and me, making decisions about the protocol and its future developments in a decentralized manner, utilizing the $UP token as a governance currency . DAO Members propose new ideas through proposals and vote on them to help the protocol be future-proof.
