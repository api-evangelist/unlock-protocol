Until recently, Unlock DAO contributors had to rely on their popularity among fellow DAO members to receive a monthly recognition in the form of retroQF ( retro quadratic funding ) with our native governance token $UP. Often, the DAO members who were most frequently seen or known received the largest portion of that funding, while the newer or more introverted ones who put in the same or even more work received significantly less. We want DAO members to be recognized fairly for contributing value to the community and beyond, particularly in creating social media content .
