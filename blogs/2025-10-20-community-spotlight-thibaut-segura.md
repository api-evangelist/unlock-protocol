Did you know that Unlock DAO is a decentralized autonomous organization that governs the actual protocol? These are ordinary people, like you, making decisions about the protocol and its future development in a decentralized manner, using the $UP token as the governance currency . DAO Members propose new ideas through proposals and vote on them to help the protocol be future-proof.
